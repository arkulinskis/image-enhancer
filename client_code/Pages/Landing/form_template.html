How it works

Nothing runs in the cloud but the queue. The painting happens on your own PC, with an open model.

An app sends a picture

With its API key, a style and any extra words. It gets a job number and an estimated finish time straight away.

It waits its turn

Oldest first, one at a time. If the PC is off, the queue asks again every 15 minutes, or the moment it reconnects.

The PC repaints it

ComfyUI runs FLUX.2 [klein] 4B on the graphics card and sends the result back. The app polls, or gets a signed webhook.

Questions

Can anyone use it?

No. Only apps with a key from the owner can send pictures, and only the owner can open the console. There is no public upload page.

What happens when the PC is switched off?

Pictures keep arriving and wait in order. The service asks the PC again every 15 minutes, and starts straight away when the worker reconnects. Apps can always ask where their picture is and when it should be ready.

How long does a picture take?

On an 8 GB laptop graphics card, about 20 to 60 seconds for a 1-megapixel picture once the model is loaded. The estimate an app gets counts every picture ahead of it.

Where do the pictures go?

Each picture and its result are stored with the job so the app can fetch them, then deleted after 30 days (the owner can change that). Nothing is sent to an outside AI service: the model runs on the owner's PC.
